Exhibition

[Objects of Home]

Solo Exhibition

This exhibition presents a collection of still life paintings that observe the quiet moments of an Indian home in Britain. The work explores how the familiar objects within these spaces - from the kitchen to the pooja space - define a sense of place.

Enjoyment can be found in everyday arrangements. A draped cloth, a specific vessel, or even the placement of a fruit speaks to a subtle, unspoken geometry. These objects are not merely things; they form a hidden scaffolding that organises our daily rituals. They hold stories that are both personal and collective.

The selection features items that have travelled across continents and others that have been acquired and adapted. Through these paintings, the work considers how a unique dual heritage is built and inhabited. It's an invitation to see the value and emotional weight held within these simple forms, and to recognise them as the quiet architects of identity.
